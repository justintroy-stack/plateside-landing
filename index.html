Why this exists

"Eat better" isn't an instruction. It's a homework assignment with no due date.

The gap

A panel comes back, something's flagged, and the plan is "watch your sugar" or "cut back on red meat." True, and useless at the grocery store — and the paid tools that promise something more personal often land in the same place. Nobody hands you the shopping list that goes with it.

The cost

You already paid for the blood work — a panel, a copay, sometimes hundreds out of pocket. The number is expensive. What it should change for dinner has been free advice, which is why it's usually vague.

Your data

Your results stay yours.

Nothing you type or upload ever leaves your phone — including a lab report. No account. No server. Whoever builds this can't see your results, because there's no place to see them from.
