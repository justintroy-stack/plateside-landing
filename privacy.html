Privacy Policy

This page covers the early-access site at plateside.app — the page you're reading, with the example kitchen and the sign-up form. It doesn't cover the Plateside app itself, which isn't public yet and works differently: nothing typed or uploaded into the app, including a lab report, ever leaves your phone. There's no account and no server for it, so there's nothing of yours for that policy to describe yet — this page is about the much smaller thing this site actually does today.

What this site collects

If you use the "pick what's flagged" demo without signing up, nothing is collected — it runs entirely in your browser and nothing you click is sent anywhere.

If you submit the early-access form, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • What you said you'd expect to pay, if you answered that question (optional)
  • Which example flags you picked and how far you logged into the example cycle, since that's what the "based on what you tried" line on the confirmation is built from

That's the whole list. No lab data, no health information, no real names, no location — the demo runs on an invented example kitchen, never anything you'd type as real.

What we don't do

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking scripts on this page
  • We never sell or share your email with anyone
  • Nothing is collected automatically — only what you type into the form and choose to submit

What it's used for

To tell you when Plateside is ready for real people to use, and to send you the example list you built on the demo, if you asked for early access. Nothing else. If we ever add anything beyond that — like an occasional email about progress — this page will say so first.

Where it's stored

In a private list only the person building Plateside can access. Not sold, not shared, not used for advertising.

Deleting your information

Email justintroy@gmail.com and ask — it'll be removed. No account or login is needed to make that request, since none exists.

Questions

Email justintroy@gmail.com directly. One person is building this, and that's who you'll reach.
